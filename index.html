Who We Are

BUILT ON PRECISION
DRIVEN BY RESULTS

9+ Years in
the Industry

M&J Demolition and Remodeling LLC was founded with the purpose of providing organized, safe, and professional demolition and renovation solutions. Led by Enrique Rivera, the company is committed to maintaining clean job sites, responsible practices, and detailed craftsmanship in every project.

We understand that every demolition or remodel is the first step toward something new, and we take that responsibility seriously — ensuring each phase is handled with care and expertise, from residential homes to commercial buildings across Texas.

Mission

To deliver safe, efficient, and high-quality demolition and remodeling services that prepare and transform spaces while exceeding client expectations.

Vision

To be recognized as a trusted leader in demolition and remodeling services in Texas, known for reliability, professionalism, and excellence.
